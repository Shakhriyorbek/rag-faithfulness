The recommendations above say how to report a faithfulness result. They do not say which evaluator to run, and nothing in this paper supports a single answer to that. The three differ on properties that matter different amounts in different applications. Table~\ref{tab:choose} collects what we measured into the form that decision needs. Every entry comes from a table earlier in the paper; none of it is new evidence. The rows are grouped by what was measured and on which answers, because the four quantities do not share a unit or a population and cannot be traded against each other.

Read it against the deployment. If answers are short and the question is whether a fabricated value gets caught, the deterministic check dominates, though the evaluator still matters: on the tersest generator's Natural Questions answers NLI-max catches 56.6\% of falsified values, AlignScore 75.4\% and MiniCheck 97.5\%. If answers are long, none of the three main-text evaluators catches most fabrications. The gap between the best and worst of them is still the difference between a weak gate and no gate: 25.1\% under claim-min and 24.1\% under AlignScore, against 5.1\% under NLI-max, on the verbose generator's NQ cell. The fact-checker catches 82.7\% there, at the cost of a 7B model per answer, and it still loses 31 and 40 points when one model is prompted to write more (Appendix~\ref{app:minicheck}). If the evaluation population cannot be guaranteed free of refusals, AlignScore is the only one of the three that scores refusals below attempted answers for every generator we ran. Under the other two, refusals score above real answers for Qwen and GPT-4o-mini and far below them for Claude and Mistral-7B, so pooling them moves the average in a generator-dependent direction. If systems are being compared and the expected difference is small, the relevant property is resolving power, where AlignScore needs 0.012 to 0.037 against 0.021 to 0.061 for NLI-max at the same sample size.

Those criteria do not agree, and we are not proposing a weighting for them. The point of stating them together is that a choice is being made whenever a faithfulness number is reported, and it is usually made by default.
