\movedsub{The effect is not verbatim copying}
\label{sec:copying}

A natural explanation is that one generator quotes the retrieved text back, so the evaluator scores a copy of its own premise and one substituted value cannot move it. The data does not support it. Verbatim overlap with the retrieved context is 0.226 for Claude, 0.194 for Qwen, 0.165 for Mistral-7B and 0.235 for GPT-4o-mini. The explanation needs that overlap to run opposite to sensitivity, and it does not: the generator that copies most is the most sensitive, and the one that copies least sits in the middle. The correlations between overlap and the response to falsification are +0.046, +0.178, +0.046 and +0.166, where the explanation needs all four to be negative. The separation also survives at matched overlap, in every band including the one above 0.3 (Appendix~\ref{app:copying}).
